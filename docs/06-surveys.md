# 06 - Surveys

## 1. Customer Expectations Survey

### Target Audience
Potential customers of Afsheen by Sheikh across Bangladesh

### Key Findings

#### Product Interest
| Category | Interest Level |
|----------|---------------|
| Heritage Clothing (Sarees, Lehengas, Bridal) | High |
| Bengali Couple & Family Sets | High |
| Fine Jewelry (Jhumkas, Bridal Sets, Hair Accessories) | High |
| Imported Skincare (Korean, Japanese, French) | Medium-High |
| Indian & Pakistani Party Dresses | Medium |
| Gowns | Medium |

#### Shopping Preferences
- **Payment:** Cash on Delivery (dominant), bKash/Nagad (growing), Card (secondary)
- **Delivery:** Expect 24-48 hours in Dhaka, 2-3 days nationwide; free delivery above ৳2,000
- **Gift Wrapping:** High demand for premium gift packaging
- **Price Sensitivity:** Premium segment (BDT 2,200 – 52,000)

#### Feature Priorities
1. Product search and category filtering — **Critical**
2. Product images and descriptions — **Critical**
3. Shopping cart and simple checkout — **Critical**
4. Flash sales with countdown timers — **Important**
5. Order tracking with visual progress — **Important**
6. Customer reviews and ratings — **Important**
7. Wishlist functionality — **Important**
8. Live chat support — **Important**
9. Size guide information — **Important**
10. Coupon/discount codes — **Nice to have**

## 2. Technical Survey

### Browser Usage (Bangladesh)
- Chrome: 65%
- Safari: 15%
- Firefox: 10%
- Samsung Internet: 8%
- Other: 2%

### Device Usage
- Mobile: 70%
- Desktop: 25%
- Tablet: 5%

### Implication
Mobile-first responsive design is essential with a bottom navigation bar for mobile users. The application must perform well on low-bandwidth connections common in Bangladesh.
