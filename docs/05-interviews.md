# 05 - Interviews

## 1. Brand Owner Interview (Sheikh Afsheen)

**Date:** July 2026
**Role:** Admin user, business owner

### Key Questions & Responses

**Q: What are the main products?**
A: Heritage Bengali couture (Jamdani, Banarasi Katan, Rajshahi Silk sarees), Bengali couple and family sets, gowns, lehengas, Indian and Pakistani party dresses, fine filigree jewelry (jhumkas, bridal sets, hair accessories), and imported skincare (Korean, Japanese, French brands).

**Q: What payment methods do you support?**
A: Cash on Delivery is primary. We also support bKash, Nagad, Visa, Mastercard, and Rocket.

**Q: What are your delivery capabilities?**
A: We use courier services. Within Dhaka: 24-48 hours. Other districts: 2-3 business days. Free delivery on orders above ৳2,000.

**Q: Do you offer promotions?**
A: Yes, we run flash sales with countdown timers. We have multiple coupon codes — GOLDEN15 (15% off), WELCOME10 (10% off), BRIDAL5000 (৳5,000 off on orders above ৳15,000), and FESTIVE20 (20% off).

**Q: How do you handle customer support?**
A: We have a live chat widget on the site. Moderators can respond to customer inquiries in real-time through the moderator dashboard.
