# 03 - Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Primary Stakeholders

### Brand Owner (Sheikh Afsheen)
- **Role:** Business owner, admin user
- **Interest:** Revenue growth, brand expansion, operational efficiency
- **Access:** Admin dashboard — full product/order/user management, coupon management
- **Contact:** admin@afsheen.com, +880 1819-888990

### Customers
- **Role:** End users browsing and purchasing products
- **Interest:** Easy browsing, secure checkout, product quality, live chat support
- **Access:** Shop, cart, checkout, customer dashboard, wishlist, live chat
- **Locations:** Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, and other districts

## 2. Secondary Stakeholders

### Moderators (Support Staff)
- **Role:** Live chat customer support
- **Interest:** Quick response times, efficient issue resolution
- **Access:** Moderator dashboard with chat interface and message history

### Store Staff (3 Locations)
- **Role:** In-store operations, order fulfillment
- **Interest:** Streamlined order processing from online channel
- **Locations:** Gulshan Flagship Lounge, Dhanmondi Design Atelier, Tatibazar Heritage Jewellery Studio

### Courier Partners
- **Role:** Delivery of orders across Bangladesh
- **Interest:** Clear delivery addresses and order details
- **Delivery SLA:** Dhaka 24-48 hours, other districts 2-3 business days

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Engagement Level |

| Customers | Medium | High | Active Users |
| Moderators | Medium | Medium | Support Staff |
